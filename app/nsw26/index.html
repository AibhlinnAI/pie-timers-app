    What this is

    Pie Timers shows time as a pie that shrinks, so how long is left reads at a glance without anyone reading a number. This page is the New South Wales ADHD Conference programme, Sunday 18 October 2026, running through it. The clock is simulated so the day can be watched now rather than on the day itself — press Pause and drag the slider to land anywhere between the doors opening and the close.

    The two views are the same data. A delegate's phone is what someone in the room follows. The foyer screen is what a display in the venue shows, reading from across a room, with nobody touching it all day.

    Times and speakers are the ones published at myspiritedmind.com. Each finish is read off the start of the next item, the way a printed programme reads. Registration, 8:30am to 10:00am, is an open period — doors and exhibits, not something to count down. The close at 3:50pm is shown as ten minutes, an estimate to reach the published 4:00pm finish, and it says so on screen.
