      What this is

      Pie Timers shows time as a pie that shrinks, so how long is left reads at a glance without anyone reading a number. This page is the New South Wales ADHD Conference 2026, Sunday 18 October at Castle Hill RSL, as one pie for the whole day, 8:30am to 4pm. While the day is running, a bar shows the time already spent against the time still to come. Each item in the list has a small pie of its own: full before it starts, emptying while it is on, empty once it is over, and grey on any other date. The clock is simulated so the day can be watched now rather than on the day itself: drag the slider to land anywhere between the doors opening and the close.

      The two views are the same data. A delegate's phone is what someone in the room follows. The foyer screen is what a display in the venue shows, reading from across a room, with nobody touching it all day.

      Where the times come from

      • Times, speakers and session titles are taken from the program published at myspiritedmind.com, last checked on 25 September 2026.
      • The published agenda gives each item a start time and no finish. Each finish shown here is read off the start of the next item, the way a printed program reads, and says (est.) on screen for that reason.
      • Registration, 8:30am to 10am, is the window the event page gives for doors and exhibits, so it carries no (est.). Its row counts that window down like any other.
      • The close at 3:50pm is shown as ten minutes, to reach the published 4pm finish. That is arithmetic, not a published figure, so it says (est.) too.
      • On the day, once an item is over it moves down to a Finished list, so whatever is on now is the first row of the list. Nothing is reordered.
      • Times are Sydney time, UTC+11:00. New South Wales daylight saving starts on the first Sunday in October, the 4th in 2026, so 18 October falls inside it.
