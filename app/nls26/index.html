The day

    What this is

    Pie Timers shows time as a pie that shrinks, so how long is left reads at a glance without anyone reading a number. This page is the two conference days of the Neurodiversity Leadership Summit 2026 — Wednesday 11 and Thursday 12 November at Crowne Plaza Sydney Darling Harbour — with a pie running on whichever session is on, and the rest of the day underneath it. The clock is simulated so a day can be watched now rather than on the day itself: press Pause and drag the slider to land anywhere between the Welcome to Country and the close.

    The two views are the same data. A delegate's phone is what someone in the room follows. The foyer screen is what a display outside the doors shows, reading from across a room, with nobody touching it all day.

    What is published, and what is not

    • The agenda is the one published at theleadershipinstitute.com.au, read off the two Conference Day panels on 23 September 2026.
    • No finish times are published for any item. Every finish shown here is read off the start of the next item, the way a printed programme reads, and every one of them says (est.) on screen for that reason.
    • Two sessions have no speaker yet. The agenda says they will be announced, so this page says the same. No name has been guessed.
    • Day 1 ends on networking drinks with no published finish, so it shows a start and nothing to count down to. Day 2's Conference Close is a moment rather than a block.
    • The Day 1 morning is shown exactly as published, and it does not run in order. The agenda lists Morning Tea at 10:40am above a session starting at 10:20am. Rather than reorder someone else's programme or invent a time to reconcile them, this page prints all three items as published and runs no countdown across them. Everything from 12:00pm onward times normally.
    • Times are Sydney time, UTC+11:00. New South Wales daylight saving starts on the first Sunday in October — the 4th in 2026 — so both conference days fall inside it.
    • This page covers the two conference days only. The 10 November pre-conference masterclass and the 13 November post-conference workshops are not shown.
