      What this is

      Pie Timers shows time as a pie that shrinks, so how long is left reads at a glance without anyone reading a number. This page is the two conference days of the Neurodiversity Leadership Summit 2026 — Wednesday 11 and Thursday 12 November at Crowne Plaza Sydney Darling Harbour — with a pie running on whatever is on, and the rest of the day as a column of pies — full for what has not started, emptying for what is on, empty for what is done, and an empty plate on anything this page will not put a clock to. The clock is simulated so a day can be watched now rather than on the day itself: press Pause and drag the slider to land anywhere between the Welcome to Country and the close.

      The two views are the same data. A delegate's phone is what someone in the room follows. The foyer screen is what a display outside the doors shows, reading from across a room, with nobody touching it all day.

      What is published, and what is not

      • The agenda is the one published at theleadershipinstitute.com.au, read off the two Conference Day panels on 23 September 2026.
      • No finish times are published for any item. Every finish shown here is read off the start of the next item, the way a printed program reads, and every one of them says (est.) on screen for that reason.
      • Two sessions have no speaker yet. The agenda says they will be announced, so this page says the same. No name has been guessed.
      • Day 1 ends on networking drinks with no published finish, so it shows a start and nothing to count down to. Day 2's Conference Close is a moment rather than a block.
      • Items are listed in the order the agenda prints them, and are never re-sorted into clock order. Once an item has finished it moves to a Finished list after the one still running, so whatever is on is always the first row of the programme; the published order is kept inside each of the two lists. Nothing is reordered, renamed or given a time it was not published with.
      • A row this page will not time shows a grey, empty plate rather than a pie — the Welcome to Country, the networking drinks and the Day 2 close. It is the same mark the large pie uses, for the same reason, and it is not the same thing as a finished row, which is dimmed instead.
      • The Day 1 morning is shown exactly as published, and it does not run in order. The agenda lists Morning Tea at 10:40am above a session starting at 10:20am. Rather than reorder someone else's program or invent a time to reconcile them, this page prints all three items as published and does not time them one by one. The one countdown it runs across them is the stretch from the first published start to the next published start after them, 9:30am to 12:00pm, and that 12:00pm is inferred exactly the way every other finish on this page is. All three rows show that same stretch emptying, identically and without a number of their own, because which of the three is running is the part nobody outside the summit can know.
      • Times are Sydney time, UTC+11:00. New South Wales daylight saving starts on the first Sunday in October — the 4th in 2026 — so both conference days fall inside it.
      • This page covers the two conference days only. The 10 November pre-conference masterclass and the 13 November post-conference workshops are not shown.
