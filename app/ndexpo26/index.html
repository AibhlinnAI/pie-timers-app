    What this is

    Pie Timers shows time as a pie that shrinks, so how long is left reads at a glance without anyone reading a number. This page is the Neurodivergent Entrepreneurs Expo — Tuesday 29 September 2026, 10am to 2pm at Woodville Town Hall — running as one pie for the whole day, with the time already spent and the time still to come shown together underneath it, and the published programme beside it. The clock is simulated so the day can be watched now rather than on the day itself: press Pause and drag the slider to land anywhere between the doors opening and the close.

    The two views are the same data. A visitor's phone is what someone at the expo follows. The foyer screen is what a display at the door shows, reading from across a room, with nobody touching it all day.

    Where the times come from

    • Every session, speaker and time on this page is taken from the event programme published by Mingle Co, exactly as written. Nothing has been added, shortened or guessed.

    • Times are South Australian, UTC+09:30. South Australian daylight saving does not start until Sunday 4 October, so 29 September is still standard time.
