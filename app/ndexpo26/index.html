      What this is

      Pie Timers shows time as a pie that shrinks, so how long is left reads at a glance without anyone reading a number. This page is the Neurodivergent Entrepreneurs Expo — Tuesday 29 September 2026, 10am to 2pm at Woodville Town Hall — running as one pie for the whole day, with the time already spent and the time still to come shown together underneath it, and the published program beside it.
